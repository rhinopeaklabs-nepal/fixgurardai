Does FixGuard install anything on my site?

No. There is no snippet, tag or agent to add. FixGuard loads your site in its own browser the way a visitor would. Nothing changes on your end.

Does it need my password?

Never. To check pages behind a login you sign in yourself and hand FixGuard the resulting session, which is scoped, expiring and revocable. Forms containing a password, card or ID field are never submitted.

How long does an audit take?

Around 30 seconds for a single page with the seven default checks. Following your sitemap and internal links takes longer, in proportion to how many routes it finds.

Can it verify the email actually arrived?

No, and it will not claim to. FixGuard sees whether your form's request left the page and what came back. The mail your host then sends goes to you, not to FixGuard.

Is my audit private?

Yes. Audits stay private to your account. Shareable links expire on a schedule you set, and no cookie or token values are ever stored.
