How it works

From one URL to evidence you can hand over.

No snippet, no agent, no access to your code. FixGuard drives a real browser the way a visitor would, then writes down exactly what happened.

  1. 1

    Paste your address.

    One URL. Check just that page, or let FixGuard find your other routes from your sitemap and your own links.

  2. 2

    It opens the site for real.

    A real Chromium browser loads the page, watches the console and the network, submits your forms with clearly labelled test data, and follows your links. Nothing is installed on your site.

  3. 3

    You get evidence, not adjectives.

    A score out of 100, every finding with the request or selector that produced it, and a PDF for whoever fixes it.

  4. 4

    Each finding becomes a prompt.

    Scoped tightly enough that an AI builder changes one thing instead of rewriting your layout.

Coverage

What it checks.

Eleven categories, each reported with the request or selector that triggered it.

  • Forms that silently failSuccess shown, nothing sent, or a 4xx/5xx behind it.
  • Redirect loopsChains that never land, with every hop.
  • Infinite re-rendersPages that never settle.
  • Broken internal links404s and soft 404s, by the path a visitor clicks.
  • JavaScript errorsConsole errors, explained in plain words.
  • Broken imagesFiles that fail to load.
  • DNS that disagreesRecords that point in different directions.
  • TLS certificate expiryBefore it lapses, not after.
  • AccessibilityContrast, heading order, duplicate ids, tap targets, text size.
  • Mobile layoutOverflow and unusable targets at phone width.
  • Core Web VitalsLCP, CLS and blocking time, measured.
